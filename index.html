2. Information Collection and Use

Green Mouse does not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) through Thrustbound: Vertical Rise.

The game functions completely offline on your Android device:

  • No User Account: You are not required to register, create an account, provide an email address, or log in to play.
  • Local Gameplay Data Only: Game scores, altitude climb records, collected acorns/coins, shop item unlock statuses (custom squirrel skins, trails, and hats), and audio/control preferences (tap vs. tilt steering mode, music and SFX volume) are stored strictly locally on your device within the application's private sandbox storage (user://game_data.cfg).
  • No Server Communication: No gameplay progress, statistics, or hardware details are ever transmitted over the network to any external server or third party.

3. 100% Offline Application

Thrustbound: Vertical Rise operates entirely offline. All game assets, textures, illustrations, sound effects, and music tracks are packaged directly inside the application installation file (APK). The game does not connect to the internet, does not use external web services, and does not require an active network connection to function.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Thrustbound: Vertical Rise contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect, track, or share user data:

  • Zero Advertisements: The game is completely ad-free. No advertising frameworks, banners, interstitial ads, or tracking SDKs are embedded in the game.
  • Zero Analytics or Trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or any other telemetry libraries.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Because no data is collected, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or accessible by advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

5. Device Permissions and Sensor Usage

Thrustbound: Vertical Rise requires zero (0) sensitive permissions:

  • No INTERNET permission.
  • No ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission.
  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Location access (neither precise nor coarse GPS).
  • No Contacts, Phone, or SMS access.
  • No External Storage reading or writing.

Sensor Usage (Accelerometer / Gyroscope): When tilt-control mode is enabled by the player, the game reads the device's hardware accelerometer/tilt sensor in real-time solely to translate physical device tilt into horizontal steering for the player character. Sensor readings are processed instantaneously in volatile device memory (RAM) and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Thrustbound: Vertical Rise is designed for players of all ages, including children and families:

  • The game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Google Play's Families Policy.
  • We do not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable).
  • Parents and guardians can have complete confidence that their children's privacy is fully respected and protected while playing.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored game information (high scores, unlocked shop items, and settings) resides strictly in your device's private sandbox storage:

  • You can clear all local game data at any time via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Thrustbound: Vertical Rise > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the game automatically and permanently deletes all locally stored game files and preferences.

8. Security

Because Thrustbound: Vertical Rise does not collect or transmit information over the internet, your gameplay data cannot be intercepted, leaked, or accessed remotely. Data safety is guaranteed by the Android operating system's application sandbox security model.
